              redefinition
  4. 8329ee1  Rewrite SBCL resize-hash-table using sentinel-insert +
              remhash
  5. 5b12e3b  Drop vestigial sb-pcl::preinform-compiler-about-class-type
              call
  6. aa5a036  Relax SIMPLE-BASE-STRING declarations to SIMPLE-STRING

  7. 5e6bd28  Pass :search t to run-program in run-external-program
              for SBCL/CMU/Clozure/SCL
  8. 8a0897d  Add SBCL ARM64 platform detection to
